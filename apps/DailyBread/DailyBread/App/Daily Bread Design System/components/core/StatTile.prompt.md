One-line: the three-up stat row.

```jsx
<div style={{display:'flex',gap:12}}>
  <StatTile value="4" label="waiting on you" color="var(--ds-accent)" />
  <StatTile value="$12.50" label="earned this week" color="var(--ds-gold)" />
  <StatTile value="5/7" label="chores today" />
</div>
```

Labels are lowercase and plain — "earned this week", not "Earned This Week".
