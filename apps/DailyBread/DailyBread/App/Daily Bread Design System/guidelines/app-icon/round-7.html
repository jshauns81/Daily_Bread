For the vector pass

  1. Rays as trapezoids, ~30% wider at the outer end. Yours are parallel-sided, which reads as spokes; tapering reads as light. Biggest single improvement available.
  2. Drop the outline ring around the host. At 1024 it's a refinement; below 87 it fills in and muddies the edge. The solid disc is stronger anyway.
  3. Keep the fleur terminals but build them from two shapes, not five — a diamond plus a notch. They'll still read as fleurs at 180 and won't turn to mush at 40.
  4. Two hill planes, not four, and drop the hairline top edges — those are the first thing to alias into shimmer.
  5. Remove the outer bezel. iOS masks the tile and applies its own edge treatment; a drawn frame double-borders on device.
  6. Consider raising the horizon. Yours sits at about 55% and the hills take the lower 45% — a lot of tile for a supporting element. At 40% the monstrance gets room and the hills still ground it.
  7. The gold is right. Matte, no metallic gradient. Don't let anyone "improve" it with a shine.
