The one real decision

Fixed rays or one ray per chore? Toggle it above, then drag Chores today — that second slider is where the difference shows.

Fixed 8, lit proportionally. The mark has one silhouette forever, whether the day holds 3 chores or 12. Always balanced, always symmetrical, reads instantly. Cost: a ray isn't literally your chore, so the connection is thematic rather than exact.

One ray per chore. Honest and personal — that ray is the bins. But at 3 chores the mark looks sparse and broken, at 12 the rays crowd into a blur, and it can't be the App Store icon because there's no canonical form.

My recommendation: fixed 8, proportional. Keep the literal mapping for the widget, where there's room for it to be exact and where a changing shape is expected.

Notes for the vector pass

① Unlit rays must stay visible, not vanish. They're the shape of the day ahead. Hold them at ~18% — present, clearly unearned. If they disappear the mark loses its silhouette and a bad morning looks broken rather than unfinished.

② Light them outward from the centre, alternating left and right, not clockwise. Symmetry survives partial states; a clockwise sweep looks lopsided at 3 of 6.

③ The host never dims. It is given, not earned — that's the whole point, and it's also what keeps a zero-chore morning from looking like a dead icon.

④ Rays as trapezoids, wider at the outer end. Rectangles read as spokes; tapered forms read as light.

⑤ At 29pt, rely on the overall glow mass rather than counting — the difference between 2 lit and 6 lit should be legible as brightness even when individual rays aren't resolvable.
